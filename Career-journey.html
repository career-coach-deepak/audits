About this exercise

Objective: review your career journey and identify patterns; work out what has and hasn't worked for you; understand your success factors and how to use them going forward.

This exercise prompts you to look back at your career to date, the highs and lows, what you've achieved and what you've learnt. We can learn an enormous amount about who we are from where we have been. Most of us never get to do a thorough stock-take of our working lives, moving reactively from one job to the next without stopping to reflect.

This is an in-depth learning process about what has worked for you in the past and what has not, so that you can use the best elements of your past as a foundation for building your future.

Record each main job since leaving school or college (you can leave out temporary or vacation jobs) up to your current or last job. Add a card per job, then answer the reflection questions below.
