How to use this
  1. Drag each card into a column, or use the small Not / Imp / Very buttons on the card (better on mobile).
  2. Very Important = non-negotiable motivators. Important = needed to stay motivated. Not Important = doesn't really influence your work either way.
  3. Click a card name (or "details") to read its full description.
  4. Review the Important pile for anything fundamental and move it to Very Important. Then tap the ★ to mark your top ten from the Very Important pile.
  5. In the Top ten section, rate how well each value is met today (0–10) and note what you'd change to honour it more fully.
  6. Finish with the Insights questions. Use Export to keep a backup, or Print to save a PDF.
