What a career vision is & how to use this tool

A career vision is a picture of your future career as you want to create it. Use your imagination to the fullest and see where you want to be in 1, 2 or 3 years.

Go through the suggested elements and place each one in one of three groups:

Must have — super important and non-negotiable. If possible, say why. Nice to have — important, but you are a bit more flexible. Don't want — you don't want it in your future career. If possible, say why.

An element you leave unsorted simply stays out of your vision. If something you care about isn't on the list, add your own at the end of section 1.
