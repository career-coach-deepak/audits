What are strengths & how to rate them

"Underlying qualities that energize us, contribute to our personal growth and lead to peak performance." — Brewerton & Brook, 2006

Our area of greatest potential and growth actually lies within our areas of strength. Objective: understand your key strengths, understand what leaves you dissatisfied and disengaged, and gain insight on how to better leverage your strengths.

Read each of the 24 strengths and rate it by how much using it energizes you:

5 · Energizes — highly energized, motivated and satisfied 3 · Neutral — neither energized nor drained 1 · Drains — drained, weakened and dissatisfied

3 · Reflect on your strengths

Pick up to 7 of your most significant strengths (the ones that energize you most). For each, how much opportunity do you get to use it, and how could you stretch it further?

Tips for stretching / developing a strength
  • Set challenging goals that stretch you or take you outside your comfort zone.
  • Acquire new knowledge or skills.
  • Take on a new project or task that plays to the strength.
  • Teach, train, coach or mentor others.
  • Use your strengths in various combinations.
  • Use your strengths in new or different ways to manage obstacles and barriers.

4 · Reflect on your weakeners

Pick up to 7 that drain you most. For each, how much are you required to use it, and how could you reduce or eliminate it?

Tips for reducing / eliminating a weakener
  • Use your strengths to compensate.
  • Use other people's strengths to compensate.
  • Develop new behaviours with the outcome in mind.
  • Use your strengths in new or different ways to manage obstacles and barriers.
